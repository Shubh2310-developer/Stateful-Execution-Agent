# Antigravity Branding Guidelines

This document defines the core identity, logo usage, and brand personality of Antigravity to ensure visual consistency across all interfaces and communications.

## 1. Brand Personality
- **Stable**: The foundation for autonomous operations.
- **Transparent**: Never hides its reasoning or state.
- **Dynamic**: Constantly learning and evolving.
- **Professional**: Built for high-stakes enterprise work.

## 2. The Logo
The Antigravity logo represents "Stateful Autonomy."

- **The Symbol**: A geometric "A" constructed from three distinct lines, symbolizing the trio of Core, Memory, and Trace.
- **Primary Mark**: Symbol + "Antigravity" wordmark in Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Minimum Clear Space**: 50% of the symbol's height on all sides.
- **Misuse**: Do not rotate, stretch, or apply drop shadows to the logo.

## 3. Color Identity
Our primary colors are derived from the [Design System](./DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

- **Antigravity Blue** (`#3B82F6`): Represents stability and technology.
- **Antigravity Orange** (`#F97316`): Represents action and user-focus (CTA).
- **The Slate Scale**: A range of slates (`#F8FAFC` to `#0F172A`) for professional surfaces.

## 4. Typography
- **Primary Brand Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- **Primary Tech Font**: `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Usage**: Use Bold for headings to convey strength, and Regular for body text for clarity.

## 5. Tone of Voice
Refer to [UX Writing Guidelines](./UX_WRITING.md) for detailed messaging patterns.

- **Concise**: "Your task is complete," not "I have successfully finished the task you requested."
- **Helpful**: Always provides a next step or a solution.
- **Data-Driven**: Cites evidence and reasoning.

## 6. Visual Motifs
- **The Grid**: Use subtle grid patterns in marketing or background surfaces to emphasize "Structure."
- **Layering**: Use clean borders and subtle depth to convey a "Professional Tool" aesthetic.
- **Motion**: Use the [Animation Specs](./ANIMATION_SPECS.md) to ensure all motion feels weighted and purposeful.
