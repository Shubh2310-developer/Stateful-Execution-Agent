# Antigravity Mission Debrief UX

A Mission Debrief is an automatically generated summary of a completed task, providing stakeholders with high-level value realization and operators with optimization data.

## 1. The Executive Summary (Artifact)
- **Outcome Card**: A high-impact card showing the primary goal and a "Success" badge.
- **Key Achievements**: A list of the top 3-5 milestones reached during the mission.
- **Value Statement**: "This mission saved 4.2 hours of manual work and utilized $0.85 in compute."

## 2. The Operational Audit
- **Execution Timeline**: A Gantt-style chart showing the duration of each plan step.
- **Tool Efficiency**: A breakdown of which tools were most effective vs. those that caused delays or errors.
- **Constraint Compliance**: A checklist confirming all user constraints were followed.

## 3. The Learning Summary
- **Memory Growth**: A list of new preferences or facts learned during the task.
- **Feedback impact**: (If refined) Showing how user feedback directly improved the final outcome.

## 4. Retrospective Analytics
- **Confidence Trend**: A line graph of the agent's confidence score throughout the mission.
- **Error Breakdown**: Categorization of any transient vs. critical errors encountered.

## 5. Next Steps & Continuation
- **Proactive Recommendations**: "Based on this mission, I suggest we run a follow-up analysis on [X]."
- **Template Promotion**: "This execution path was highly efficient. Save as a template?"

## 6. Visual Presentation
- **Print-ready PDF**: A beautifully formatted version of the debrief for email or board reporting.
- **Interactive Replay**: A "Rewind" button to see a high-speed video of the [State Visualization](./STATE_VISUALIZATION.md) graph evolving during the mission.
